Shijing rare-character webfonts

These renamed WOFF2 subsets contain only the rare Unicode characters
used in the reviewed records and full-book work index. Outlines are unchanged. They provide
a readable font, not a facsimile or an independent textual witness.

Original: Hanazono Mincho A/B, AFDKO edition, version 8.030

Regenerate with scripts/build-shijing-pilot-font.py using fonttools[woff]
and brotli, with the two official OTFs in .cache/modern-shijing.
manifest.json records source URLs, SHA-256 hashes, and character coverage.
Do not substitute these glyph outlines for a source page's precise glyph form.
